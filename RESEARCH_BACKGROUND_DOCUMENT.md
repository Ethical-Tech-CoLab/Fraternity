# RESEARCH BACKGROUND DOCUMENT

Foundational document for the DPI research tool. This file exists to
support the research in general — it is the entry point for anyone
picking up this work, not a finding in itself.

## Phase

This work is currently in the **Theory of Change** phase: broad-to-narrow
evidence gathering, not yet narrowed to a single intervention and not yet
building anything. See `CONCEPT.md` for the full methodology (four
pathways, sourcing discipline, and the criteria — high-leverage,
tractable, neglected — that will eventually justify narrowing down).

## What exists so far

- `CONCEPT.md` — the reusable research methodology.
- `docs/cases/` — five comparative case files (Dzaleka, Kakuma, Cox's
  Bazar, Za'atari, Bidibidi), each following the same four-pathway
  structure so they stay comparable.
- `../dzaleka-cost-model/docs/` — 22 deep-dive research documents specific
  to Dzaleka Refugee Camp, Malawi (legal/policy, funding, disaster risk,
  protection, actor landscape, family/vulnerable groups, health, and
  claim verification), compiled September 2026. This is where most of
  the current evidence base lives.
- A compiled synthesis of the Dzaleka-specific research, clustered
  against the narrowing criteria, exists as a Claude Doc:
  https://claude.ai/code/artifact/cf8a4a89-d5ca-43ee-b9bd-69986b1d04b2

## Sourcing discipline

Same as everywhere else in this project: every figure keeps its original
source, date, and reporting period. Conflicting figures are kept side by
side, not reconciled. Single-sourced or non-mainstream claims are flagged
as unverified rather than treated as fact.

## Status

Research phase only. No tool or public-facing product has been built yet.
