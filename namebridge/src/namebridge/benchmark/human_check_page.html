Pair check: same person or not?

300 pairs from the development set of the NameBridge synthetic benchmark. Everyone here is invented. For each pair, decide as a caseworker would: are these two records about the same person?

How to judge a pair
  • Same person: you would treat the two records as one person, despite spelling, order or age differences.
  • Different people: they look like two people (another person with a similar name, a relative, a different age or sex).
  • Can't tell: the records do not give enough to decide. This is a valid answer, not a failure.
  • Tick Looks unrealistic when a record could not occur in real life (an impossible name, age or place). Add a note if you can say why.
  • Ages are as recorded: "about 20 (in 2017)" means the record, made in 2017, gave an age of about 20. A tracing request describes the sought person as a relative remembers them.
